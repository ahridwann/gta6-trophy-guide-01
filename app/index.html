    Roadmap to the platinum: Vice Got a Price!

    Five phases, about 90–120 hours in total. The estimate follows GTA V's shape — its base-game platinum took 200–300 hours with 18 Online trophies; this list has no Online trophies and 55 entries, so the road is shorter but still a real grind. Do the phases in order: the story first, the timed windows while they are open, the grinds last.

    1. Phase 1 — Play the story (Chapters 1–2) ≈ 15–20 h · 9 trophies

      Finish Gone Fishing and Chapters 1–2, plus the named missions as they come (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play). Start every stranger strand the moment its icon appears — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each have a final-mission trophy, and strands are the one thing we cannot promise stays open after the finale. Try each activity type once for Master of None.

      Trophies: Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Trust, Break the Bank, Family Matters, plus strand starts.

    2. Phase 2 — Vice City and the middle chapters (Chapters 3–4) ≈ 20–25 h · 10 trophies

      Finish Chapters 3–4 and the remaining named missions (Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory). Finish all five stranger strands and the Strangers & Freaks silver. Do both partner trophies now — Hot Together and Retail Therapy need your partner beside you, and partner missions are frequent mid-story. Make every choice the game offers for Actions Speak Louder (PSN counts 500).

      Trophies: Vice City, Baby!, Top Of The World, Ma, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked, Snow Storm, all five strand finals, Strangers & Freaks, Hot Together, Retail Therapy.

    3. Phase 3 — Before the finale: the checkpoint ≈ 1 h · 0 trophies, saves the run

      Stop before the final mission and check this guide's tracker. Every trophy flagged WATCH should be ticked by now — all stranger strands, both partner trophies, and the choice count well under way. This is the last safe checkpoint: in GTA V, four trophies died with the wrong ending choice, and while GTA VI publishes no such choice, a finale is still the one point where side content can close.

      Checkpoint: 9 WATCH trophies earned. If any is missing, do it now.

    4. Phase 4 — Finale and the open world ≈ 25–35 h · 20 trophies

      Finish the final mission (gold), then sweep the open world with the whole map open: explore all 52 areas, discover 100 vehicles, meditate at 18 spots, WAiNK It at 100 locations, complete 3 card sets, rob 5 bank vaults and an armored truck, hit one illegal operation per gang, launder $250,000, buy all 8 garages and register 10 vehicles to Scooter Bros. Hunt Bret's unique animal, beat Cal at fishing, bowl at Kolt & Associates and have a cocktail on Ocean Beach.

      Trophies: You Gotta Know When to Get Off, The Goodtime State, Whip It Good, Positive Mental Attitude, You WAiNKer!, Don't Touch My Deck, Maximum Withdrawal, A Classic Blitz Play, Pest Control, Dime on the Dollar, Little Boxes, On the Spot, Fair Game, Baited My Tackle, Let’s Go Bowling, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Ten Pin, Sneakthief, Catch Me If You Can.

    5. Phase 5 — The grinds and the platinum ≈ 25–35 h · 8 trophies

      Last come the long totals: 250 stunts, gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges (the hardest skill trophy), wins at all 14 drone-race locations, 20 different race wins, 50 challenges, 5 enemies in one Combat Focus, the tank trophy, and the last choices. When the tracker reads 54 of 55, the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — pops, and with it A Long Road for 100% completion.

      Trophies: Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash, Not Kudos, One Last Thing, Gunslinger, Challenging Tank, Actions Speak Louder, then Vice Got a Price! and A Long Road.

    The missables, plainly

    No trophy is confirmed missable by Rockstar — the official descriptions do not say so. The 9 trophies flagged WATCH in this guide are our reading of the descriptions: stranger strands (5 finals + the 8-strand silver) and partner trophies (2) depend on people and moments the story may take away, and the choice trophy counts decisions that may not come back. Finish them during the story and the question never arises. Everything else can be done after the finale, in free roam.
