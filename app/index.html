      About this guide

      Leonida Legends covers all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official ones as published by PlayStation. Every guide is written in our own words for this guide and names its sources beside it — the official description, Exophase, PSNProfiles or PowerPyx. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork, box art or game screenshot is used anywhere; the design is our own. The app is static files only and works with the network off: it fetches nothing at run time.
